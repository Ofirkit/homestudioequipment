## Quick verdict

The **Akai MPK Mini IV** packs keys, pads, knobs, a color screen and real pitch and mod wheels into a small, USB-powered controller with a large sound bundle. Owners love the wheels, the portability and the included sounds. The most common complaints are software and DAW setup problems, and keys that some find stiff or cheap-feeling.

## Should you buy the MPK Mini IV?

**Yes,** if you want a compact, portable controller with keys, pads and knobs, and a large sound library to start with. **No,** if you want a soft, light key feel, need more than 25 keys, or expect it to work perfectly with no setup.

## What's new in the MPK Mini IV?

- **Pitch and mod wheels** replace the previous model's joystick. Owners who upgraded call this the biggest improvement.
- **Gen-3 keybed,** which Akai describes as premium and more expressive.
- **Full-color screen and push/turn encoder** for reaching settings, presets and DAW functions without going to the computer.
- **USB-C,** replacing the previous model's USB-B connection.
- **Expanded transport section,** pre-mapped to DAWs.

## What's in the box and the software bundle

- MPK Mini IV, quickstart guide and USB cable
- Studio Instrument Collection: 1,000+ drums, keys, synths, bass and FX from AIR, Akai Professional and Moog (VST/AU and standalone)
- Ableton Live Lite 12
- One of three Native Instruments sound bundles (NKS support)
- 30-day Melodics trial and 2 months of Splice

*Bundle details are from Akai's listing and may change.*

## What owners like

- **The wheels.** Owners coming from the joystick version and from the Arturia MiniLab 2 call the pitch and mod wheels a game changer. Another owner says the feel and look are the best of any MPK Mini version yet.
- **Editing without a computer.** One owner liked setting up the pads and controls straight from the front panel and saving custom setups to the controller's memory, and found the manual clearly written.
- **MIDI Out for older gear.** One owner uses the 5-pin MIDI Out to control older keyboards and drum machines.
- **Portability.** Owners repeatedly mention how light and compact it is, whether for a backpack or a crowded desk.
- **The bundled sounds.** Several owners praised the quality of the Studio Instrument Collection, with one comparing it to Arturia's Analog Lab.
- **Easy setup for many.** Owners report smooth setups with Ableton, Logic, FL Studio and Cubase, and one said it connected to their Mac easily.
- **Good for beginners.** Two owners recommend it to beginners, and one says it helped them make music they're happy with within a few weeks.
- **Arpeggiator and Chord modes,** which one owner found easy to use from the menus.
- **Adjustable key response.** Owners can choose between three velocity curves.

## Common complaints

**Software and setup (the most common theme)**
- One owner found it didn't work as a control surface with Logic Pro 12. Akai support confirmed the problem and said a fix was in progress, with no date (March 2026).
- Two owners had reliability problems in Ableton, including issues in DAW mode on macOS. Akai's manual includes troubleshooting steps, and one owner found that removing the Akai driver helped.
- One owner reported the Studio Instrument plugin crashing and having display problems in Reaper and Waveform. Another couldn't figure out how to use the bundled sounds inside their DAW.
- One owner's serial number had already been redeemed, so they couldn't download the included software.
- One owner said a firmware update stopped the controller from sending MIDI.
- One owner's unit came without a manual or link to one. Another found setup a pain, and a third returned it because it was too hard to get working.

**Keys**
- Several owners found the keys stiff, needing a firm press, or cheap-feeling. One preferred the Arturia MiniLab 3's keybed.
- One owner noted mini keys are a compromise for bigger hands.

**Build and reliability**
- Some owners say the build leans plastic. One had a single key that wobbled and made noise.
- One owner's unit stopped working after almost a year.

**Other**
- One owner found the pads a bit laggy.
- One owner wished they could hear the sounds assigned to the pads while editing. You only hear them after saving the setup.
- One owner wished it had an on/off switch.
- One owner got a lot of marketing emails after downloading the included software.
