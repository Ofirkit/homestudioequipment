## Quick verdict

The **JBL 305P MkII** is a pair of powered studio monitors with a 5-inch woofer and 1-inch tweeter in each speaker, made for mixing, editing and listening at a desk. Owners praise the clear, neutral sound, better-than-expected bass for the size, a wide stereo image and the value for the price. The most common complaints are a quiet background hiss that some owners hear up close, and speakers that arrived faulty or failed within days.

## Should you buy the JBL 305P MkII?

**Yes,** if you want your first or an upgraded pair of studio monitors for a desk or small room and already have, or plan to get, an audio interface. **No,** if you're very sensitive to hiss when sitting close, need to plug into a receiver with speaker wire or RCA, or want something louder for a bigger room.

## What owners like

- **Clear, neutral sound.** Many owners describe the sound as clear, honest and uncolored. A dialogue editor says it won't flatter a bad edit, and a performer says it caught a small mistake in their sound that they then heard live.
- **More bass than expected.** Several owners were surprised by the low end for a 5-inch woofer. One owner says the bass is well balanced and not overpowering, and another says it doesn't reach the deepest bass but still has a real rumble with EDM.
- **Wide stereo image.** Owners mention a tight, consistent stereo image, and one says it stays consistent even when you're not sitting dead center.
- **Clear at any volume.** One owner likes the clarity at low volumes, and another says they stay clear even very loud.
- **Value.** Owners repeatedly call them great value, including first-time monitor buyers.
- **Room tuning.** Owners use the Boundary EQ and HF Trim switches to adjust for their room. One owner found settings that worked after some tweaking.
- **Upgrades over other speakers.** Owners coming from the PreSonus Eris 3.5, Klipsch ProMedia 2.1, KRK monitors and another brand with amp problems describe a clear step up.
- **Good with a subwoofer.** Two owners who added a sub, one a JBL LSR310S, loved the combination.
- **Many uses.** Owners use them for mixing, mastering, dialogue editing, song demos, voiceover, live-show prep, a digital piano, home office meetings and music listening.

## Common complaints

**Background hiss**
- Five owners hear a hiss from the speakers, some even with nothing connected. It's most noticeable when sitting close on a desk or during quiet music.
- One owner says the hiss doesn't increase with the volume knob, and that one notch on the knob gives off a faint high-pitched sound. Another says higher volumes make the hiss worse.
- Some owners don't find it disruptive. One owner bought them anyway after reading about the hiss and called them unusable.

**Faulty or failing speakers**
- Eight owners had a problem with one or both speakers. These include a speaker that failed within 24 hours and only rumbled, one that blew after a few days, one that wouldn't power on, one with low output, one that was muffled, and buzzing or rattling at certain notes or frequencies.
- One owner bought a second pair and found all four speakers had odd sounds at certain volumes or frequencies. Almost two years later, they say the sound is subtle, they don't notice it, and they've come to really like the speakers.

**Quality control and build**
- One owner lists a range of issues across units: power LEDs with different brightness, bubbles in the finish, a crooked bezel, scratches and missing adhesive feet. They still recommend the speakers.
- One owner received both speakers with scratched front panels.
- One owner says the rear switches are flimsy, and the -10/+4 switch snapped off.

**Missing items**
- Five owners say only one speaker arrived even though the listing is for a pair. All of these reviews are from 2021 and 2022.
- One owner got two speakers but only one power cord.

**Setup and controls**
- Each speaker needs its own power outlet and has its own power switch on the back. One owner wished the power cables were longer.
- One owner finds the rear volume knob awkward to reach. Another wishes there were a standby mode.
- One owner couldn't connect them to their AV receiver or desktop computer, because there are no RCA or speaker-wire inputs.
- One owner returned them because they were bigger than expected.
- One owner wished there were an easy way to mount them on a wall.

**Sound preferences**
- One owner found the mids lacking for jazz and new age music.
- One owner finds the bass a bit much for their room, even after using the Boundary EQ.
- One owner found them not loud enough and preferred cheaper generic monitors.

**Shipping and support**
- Several owners had trouble with delivery, returns or support. One waited 6 weeks for delivery, one was still waiting for a refund a month after returning them, and one says UPS charged $20 to package and ship them back, so keep the box.
