## Which interface is best for most beginners?

The **Scarlett 2i2 (4th Gen)** is the safest pick. It sets your input level for you and helps prevent clipping, so you spend less time fiddling and more time recording.

## Which interface has the best metering?

The **MOTU M2**. Its full-color LCD meters show your input and output levels at a glance, which is handy when you're gain-staging a mix or going live.

## Which interface adds the most character?

The **UA Volt 2**. Flip on Vintage mode and the preamp adds a warm, tube-like color that flatters vocals and acoustic instruments.

## Who should buy which?

- **Recording your first vocals or podcast?** Get the Scarlett 2i2.
- **Producing electronic music and streaming?** Get the MOTU M2.
- **Singer-songwriter who wants a warmer sound?** Get the Volt 2.
