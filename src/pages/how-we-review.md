# How We Review

We want you to know exactly where our information comes from, so you can decide how much weight to give it.

## Where our information comes from

Each review draws on two sources:

1. **Official manufacturer information.** Specs, features, what's in the box and bundled software come from the manufacturer's own product details.
2. **Owner reviews.** We collect real owner reviews on Amazon, both positive and negative, and use AI to find the patterns: what people like, what they complain about, and who the product seems to suit.

## Why we use AI

Reading dozens of reviews for every product takes hours. AI lets us go through all of them and spot what owners mention again and again, so you get the patterns without doing that research yourself.

## What we don't do

- **We don't claim to have tested a product unless we have.** If a review is based on owner feedback, we say so at the top of the post.
- **We don't make up specs or features.** If the manufacturer or owners don't mention something, we leave it out.
- **We don't copy reviews word for word.** We summarize them in our own words.
- **We don't overstate what owners say.** When a point comes from a single owner, we say "one owner," not "many."

## Hands-on reviews

When we have used a product ourselves, the post will clearly say so, and our own experience will be marked separately from owner feedback.

## How we make money

We earn a commission when you buy through our Amazon links, at no extra cost to you. That never decides what goes into a review. We include the complaints as well as the praise. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Spotted something wrong?

Specs and bundles change. If you see something outdated or incorrect, [contact us](/contact/) and we'll fix it.
